Our story

From South India to Surat

A café inspired by temple-town breakfasts, brass filter coffee, banana-leaf meals and the joyful colors of South India.

Simple food. Big comfort.

At anna_ldlli, we celebrate classic South Indian flavors while giving them a modern café setting. Our batter is fermented with care, chutneys are prepared fresh, and every cup of filter coffee is brewed for that unmistakable aroma.
